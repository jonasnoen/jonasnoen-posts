## Patterns of the week

1. **"AI did this annoying job for me" as a result-first hook.** The strongest AI tutorials open with a finished,
   concrete result in one short verb sentence (AI cleaned up / sorted / turned X into Y for me), then show the
   steps. Messy data (inbox, photos, files) and personal privacy were the recurring tasks.
2. **Keyword comment → the exact prompt / full breakdown.** Many independent outliers end with "comment WORD and
   I'll send you the prompt(s)". Works best when the video shows the result and the DM promises *more* than the
   video (all prompts, step-by-step). Confirms our PROMPT flow – keep the guide clearly bigger than the reel.
3. **Privacy warnings perform.** "Your AI chats / your data are exposed unless you do this" style hooks with an
   urgent but practical fix did well across several creators. Good field for honest, fact-checked prompts.
4. **Sounding right at work.** Tone and wording at work (polite vs. blunt, what to say to your boss, what an
   interviewer really means) worked in skits and in two-column "what they say | what they mean" tables –
   a natural fit for prompts that return several tone versions or a decoder table.
