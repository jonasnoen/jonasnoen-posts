## Patterns of the week

1. **"Stop prompting like this – do X instead."** Several independent outliers attack the way people prompt and
   offer a structure as the fix: let the AI interview you first, let it rewrite your prompt, think in goals instead of
   long instructions. Strong fit for a "prompt that builds your prompt" guide.
2. **"Make AI stop sounding like AI."** Hooks about AI-sounding text (robotic phrasing, tell-tale patterns) plus a
   concrete fix did well with more than one creator. Honest angle for us: a rewrite prompt in *your* voice – no
   promises about beating detectors.
3. **Named "codes" for one everyday job.** Short lists of 3–4 named commands/prompts for a single task (most clearly:
   turning a book into notes) keep working – our "Claude Codes" series format is right on trend.
4. **Keyword comment → step-by-step guide, result first.** Still the dominant CTA. The best ones open with the
   finished result or a surprising capability, then show the steps; the DM promises the full setup. Confirms our
   PROMPT flow and the "first 3 seconds" rule.
